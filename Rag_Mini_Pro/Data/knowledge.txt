NEURALPATH AI INSTITUTE - INSTITUTE POLICY
==========================================
A fictional policy document.
Effective date: 1 January 2027 | Version 1.0
Issued by: Office of the Academic Director


1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
--------------------
This policy sets out the rules and expectations for all students enrolled at
NeuralPath AI Institute ("the Institute"). By enrolling, every student agrees
to follow the policies below. The Institute may update this policy with 14
days' written notice.


2. COURSES
----------
The Institute offers the following programs:

  Course                              Duration   Mode                 Level
  ----------------------------------  ---------  -------------------  ------------
  AI Foundations                      8 weeks    Classroom / Online   Beginner
  Python for Data & AI                10 weeks   Classroom / Online   Beginner
  Machine Learning Practitioner       16 weeks   Classroom / Hybrid   Intermediate
  Deep Learning & Neural Networks     16 weeks   Classroom / Hybrid   Intermediate
  Generative AI & Prompt Engineering  12 weeks   Online               Intermediate
  Advanced AI Engineer Program        28 weeks   Hybrid               Advanced

2.1 Eligibility. Beginner courses are open to anyone aged 16 or above.
    Intermediate courses require completion of Python for Data & AI or a basic
    programming assessment. The Advanced AI Engineer Program requires
    completion of Machine Learning Practitioner or equivalent experience,
    verified by interview.

2.2 Batch size. Each batch is limited to 30 students to ensure personal
    attention.

2.3 Course changes. Curriculum content may be updated to reflect industry
    developments. Students will be notified of significant changes at least
    one week in advance.


3. FEES
-------
  Course                              Total Fee (INR)
  ----------------------------------  ---------------
  AI Foundations                      18,000
  Python for Data & AI                22,000
  Machine Learning Practitioner       55,000
  Deep Learning & Neural Networks     60,000
  Generative AI & Prompt Engineering  45,000
  Advanced AI Engineer Program        1,20,000

3.1 Payment options. Fees may be paid in full at enrollment (a 5%
    early-payment discount applies) or in up to three installments: 50% at
    enrollment, 25% at the end of the first third of the course, and 25% at
    the end of the second third.

3.2 Late payment. Installments overdue by more than 7 days attract a late fee
    of Rs. 500. Access to the learning portal may be suspended if payment is
    more than 15 days overdue.

3.3 Refunds.
    - Cancellation within 3 days of enrollment: full refund, less a Rs. 1,000
      registration charge.
    - Cancellation before the second week ends: 50% refund.
    - No refunds after the second week, except in cases of documented medical
      emergency, which the Director will review individually.

3.4 Discounts. A 10% merit scholarship is available to students scoring above
    90% in the entrance assessment. Group enrollments of three or more receive
    a 7% discount per student. Discounts cannot be combined.

3.5 Not included. Fees do not cover personal laptops, optional cloud credits
    beyond the allotted quota, or re-examination fees.


4. TIMINGS
----------
4.1 Regular batches

  Batch    Days                Time
  -------  ------------------  ---------------------------------------------
  Morning  Monday - Friday     9:00 AM - 12:00 PM
  Evening  Monday - Friday     6:00 PM - 9:00 PM
  Weekend  Saturday & Sunday   10:00 AM - 4:00 PM (45-minute lunch break)

4.2 Lab and doubt-clearing hours. The AI lab is open Monday to Saturday,
    8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Faculty hold doubt-clearing sessions every weekday
    from 12:30 PM to 1:30 PM.

4.3 Punctuality. Students arriving more than 15 minutes after the start of a
    session will be marked late. Three late arrivals count as one absence.

4.4 Holidays. The Institute follows the Gujarat state public holiday
    calendar. Missed sessions due to Institute closure will be rescheduled
    and announced in advance.

4.5 Batch changes. A student may request a batch change once per course,
    subject to seat availability and approval by the Academic Coordinator.


5. ATTENDANCE
-------------
5.1 Minimum requirement. Students must maintain at least 80% attendance in
    each course to be eligible for the final assessment and certificate.

5.2 Recording. Attendance is recorded at the start of each session through
    the Institute's biometric system (classroom) or login tracking (online).
    Online students must keep their cameras on for at least 75% of each
    session.

5.3 Warnings.
    - Below 85%: a reminder email is sent.
    - Below 80%: a formal warning is issued and a meeting with the Academic
      Coordinator is required.
    - Below 70%: the student may be barred from the final assessment.

5.4 Condonation. Attendance shortfalls of up to 5% may be condoned once per
    course with valid supporting documentation and the Director's approval,
    in exchange for completing additional assignments.

5.5 Make-up sessions. Recorded lectures are available for online and hybrid
    students for 30 days. Recorded sessions do not count toward attendance
    unless the absence was approved under the leave policy.


6. LEAVE
--------
6.1 Types of leave

  Leave Type                  Allowance                Documentation
  --------------------------  -----------------------  ------------------------------------
  Casual leave                Up to 3 days per course  Prior email to coordinator
  Medical leave               Up to 10 days per course Medical certificate if over 2 days
  Emergency leave             Up to 5 days per course  Written explanation within 3 days
  Internship/placement leave  As approved               Offer letter or interview call letter

6.2 Applying for leave. Leave requests should be submitted through the
    student portal at least 48 hours in advance, except in emergencies.

6.3 Approved leave and attendance. Approved leave days are excluded from the
    attendance calculation but do not exempt the student from submitting
    assignments or covering missed content.

6.4 Extended leave. Absence of more than 15 consecutive days requires
    approval from the Director. Students may be offered the option to defer
    to the next batch (valid for up to six months, once only, with a
    Rs. 2,000 administrative fee).

6.5 Unauthorized absence. Absence of more than 7 consecutive days without
    notice may result in the student's enrollment being cancelled with no
    refund.


7. PROJECTS
-----------
7.1 Overview. Projects are central to learning at the Institute. Every course
    includes hands-on work, culminating in a capstone project.

7.2 Project structure.
    - Mini projects: Short individual assignments after each module
      (30% of final grade).
    - Mid-course project: Individual or pair project applying the core
      concepts learned (20%).
    - Capstone project: A substantial, end-to-end project solving a
      real-world problem, completed individually or in teams of up to 4
      (30%). Advanced AI Engineer students must also deploy their capstone
      as a working application.
    - Final assessment: Written and practical evaluation (20%).

7.3 Mentorship. Each capstone team is assigned a faculty mentor and has
    fortnightly review meetings.

7.4 Academic integrity. All submitted work must be original. Use of AI coding
    assistants and generative tools is permitted only where the assignment
    explicitly allows it, and must always be disclosed. Plagiarism, copying
    code without attribution, or fabricating results will lead to a zero for
    that submission; repeated violations may lead to dismissal.

7.5 Data and ethics. Students must use only legally obtained, appropriately
    licensed datasets. Projects involving personal or sensitive data require
    prior approval from the faculty mentor, and must follow responsible AI
    principles including fairness, privacy, and transparency.

7.6 Intellectual property. Students retain ownership of their capstone
    projects. The Institute may showcase project summaries (without
    confidential data) for educational and promotional purposes, unless the
    student opts out in writing.

7.7 Deadlines. Late submissions lose 10% of marks per day, up to a maximum of
    5 days, after which the submission will not be accepted unless an
    extension was approved in advance.


8. CERTIFICATES
---------------
8.1 Eligibility. A certificate of completion is awarded to students who:
    - Achieve at least 80% attendance;
    - Score at least 50% overall (and at least 40% in each component);
    - Submit all required projects, including the capstone; and
    - Have cleared all outstanding fees.

8.2 Grades

  Overall Score   Grade
  --------------  -------------
  85% and above   Distinction
  70% - 84%       Merit
  50% - 69%       Pass
  Below 50%       Not Awarded

8.3 Format. Each certificate carries a unique ID and QR code that employers
    can use to verify authenticity on the Institute's website. A digital copy
    is issued within 10 working days of results; a printed copy is available
    on request at Rs. 300.

8.4 Participation certificate. Students who do not meet the full criteria but
    attended at least 60% of sessions may receive a participation certificate
    stating the modules attended.

8.5 Re-assessment. Students who fail the final assessment may reappear once
    within 60 days on payment of a Rs. 2,500 re-examination fee. Capstone
    resubmission is allowed once with a 10% mark deduction.

8.6 Revocation. The Institute reserves the right to revoke a certificate if
    it was obtained through fraud, plagiarism, or misrepresentation.

8.7 Duplicate certificates. Lost or damaged certificates may be reissued for
    Rs. 500 upon written request.


9. CODE OF CONDUCT
------------------
Students are expected to treat peers, staff, and faculty with respect, avoid
disruptive behavior, and use Institute computing resources responsibly.
Misuse of GPUs or cloud credits (for example, cryptocurrency mining),
harassment, or damage to property may lead to suspension or dismissal.


10. GRIEVANCES AND CONTACT
--------------------------
Students with concerns may raise them with the Academic Coordinator first,
then escalate to the Director if unresolved within 7 working days.
